# Conversation Notes - [role or first name], [YYYY-MM-DD]

> One sheet per conversation, filled in right after the call while it's fresh.
> Save into the project folder as `notes-YYYY-MM-DD-<who>.md`. Fixed format -
> don't improvise variations; `/gtm interviews` synthesizes these mechanically.
> Facts and exact words only - impressions go in the last field, labeled as yours.

- **Who:** [role, company shape, segment - no identifying details beyond a first name]
- **Pool:** [recent switcher / active struggler / churned user / current user / stranger]
- **Last real instance of the problem:** [what happened, when]
- **What they do about it today:** [the real alternative - tool, spreadsheet, person, nothing]
- **What it costs them:** [time / money / standing - their estimate, not yours]
- **Tried and abandoned:** [previous attempts and why each didn't stick]
- **Exact quotes:** [2-5 verbatim lines, in their words - the awkward phrasing is the asset]
- **Switch forces** (if they recently changed how they solve it):
  - push: [what made them start looking]
  - pull: [what the new way promised]
  - anxiety: [what almost stopped them]
  - habit: [what kept pulling them back]
- **Commitment given:** [time / intro / money actually given - or "compliments only"]
- **Contradicts our profile?** [anything that clashes with ICP, pains, or alternatives]
- **Your read** (opinion, not evidence): [one line]
