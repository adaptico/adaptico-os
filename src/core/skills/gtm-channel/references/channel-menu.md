# The Channel Menu - the Full Sweep List

The nineteen traction channels cataloged by the Bullseye framework (Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares, *Traction*), regrouped into twelve families a software founder can actually staff. Every family must get a one-line verdict in the Phase 1 sweep - the regrouping changes the packaging, never the coverage.

Weekly costs are honest planning figures for one founder doing it properly (practitioner consensus, not measurements). Compounding classes: **fast signal** (readable inside 4 weeks), **compounding** (assets accumulate and keep working), or both.

## The twelve families

| # | Family | Covers (canonical channels) | Who it fits | Honest weekly cost | Signal & compounding |
|---|--------|------------------------------|-------------|--------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Founder-led social** | community building (the founder's own audience) | Founders whose ICP reads X/LinkedIn; products with a story worth following | 4-8 h | Fast signal (replies land day one); compounds as the audience grows |
| 2 | **Niche communities & forums** | targeting blogs, community building | Products whose buyers ask questions in Reddit/HN/Slack/Discord niches | 3-5 h | Fast signal; reputation compounds slowly |
| 3 | **Content marketing** | content marketing | Founders who can genuinely write; categories buyers research before buying | 6-10 h per piece | Slow signal (months); compounds hard |
| 4 | **SEO** | search engine optimization | Categories with real search volume the founder can name | Lumpy: groundwork days, then 2-4 h | Slow signal; compounds hardest |
| 5 | **Cold outreach** | sales | Sales-led products with ACV that pays for the effort; founders who tolerate rejection | 4-6 h | Fast signal; never compounds - stops when you stop |
| 6 | **Launch platforms & directories** | existing platforms (launch surfaces) | Self-serve products with a demo-able "aha"; new-category tools | Spike weeks, not steady state | Fast signal burst; residual backlinks only |
| 7 | **Marketplaces & integrations** | existing platforms, business development | Products that live inside another ecosystem (app stores, plugin markets, integration galleries) | Build cost upfront, then 1-2 h | Medium; listing rank and reviews compound |
| 8 | **Engineering as marketing** | engineering as marketing | Technical founders who can ship a small free tool the ICP searches for | Dev-days upfront, then near zero | Slow-medium; the tool compounds like content |
| 9 | **Email marketing (owned list)** | email marketing | Anyone with traffic worth capturing; pairs with every other family | 1-3 h once a list exists | Compounds with list growth; needs another channel to feed it |
| 10 | **Paid acquisition** | search engine marketing, social & display ads, offline ads | Products past PMF with known unit economics and a converting funnel | Money plus 2-3 h | Fast signal; never compounds - rent, not equity |
| 11 | **Partnerships & affiliates** | business development, affiliate programs | Products with adjacent-audience allies; ACV that supports rev-share | 2-4 h, long lead times | Slow signal; a working partnership compounds |
| 12 | **PR, events & speaking** | publicity, unconventional PR, trade shows, offline events, speaking engagements | Founders with a genuinely new story or a conference-shaped ICP | Bursty and unpredictable | Spike signal; credibility compounds faintly |
| - | **Viral / referral loops** | viral marketing | Products whose users naturally invite others (collaboration, sharing) | Build cost, then low | Compounds only on top of existing users - an amplifier, not a first channel |

Viral/referral is listed for sweep completeness and is almost never the pick pre-traction: a loop multiplies users you already acquire through something else. Give it its verdict line and move on.

## Loop skeletons and handoffs (Phase 4 raw material)

The starter plan in the report adapts the picked family's skeleton to this founder's hours, communities, and angle - never restate the skeleton as the plan.

- **Founder-led social** - Week 1: profile/bio setup, watchlists, note file for raw material. Weekly loop: daily 15-20 min listening-and-reply ritual, 2-3 posts. Indicators: replies earned, profile clicks, followers who match the ICP. Handoff: `/gtm social` (and the build-in-public starter kit in the report).
- **Niche communities & forums** - Week 1: pick 3-5 communities, read the rules, earn presence without linking. Weekly loop: answer 3-7 real questions properly. Indicators: upvotes/accepted answers, profile clicks, DMs. Handoff: `/gtm social` (the listening workflow is the same muscle).
- **Content marketing** - Week 1: 5-10 title-level ideas pressure-tested against real buyer questions; pick one. Weekly loop: one piece shipped or meaningfully advanced, distributed where the ICP already is. Indicators: qualified readers, email captures, replies. Handoff: `/gtm seo` for groundwork sanity first.
- **SEO** - Week 1: run the groundwork audit; fix crawl/index blockers. Weekly loop: one groundwork fix plus one intent-matched page. Indicators: indexing, impressions on named queries. Handoff: `/gtm seo` (respect its when-to-invest verdict - if it says too early, that is scoring evidence, not an obstacle).
- **Cold outreach** - Week 1: 50-name list built on one tight ICP slice, sequences drafted. Weekly loop: 25-50 personalized sends, replies worked same-day. Indicators: reply rate, meetings booked, objections heard (they feed positioning). Handoff: `/gtm outreach`.
- **Launch platforms & directories** - Weeks 1-2: asset prep against the playbook checklist; weeks 3-4: staged launches. Indicators: day-one signups, comment quality, follow-on mentions. Handoff: `/gtm launch`.
- **Marketplaces & integrations** - Week 1: pick the one ecosystem with observed ICP presence; start the listing/integration. Weekly loop: ship toward listing, then work reviews and ranking. Indicators: installs, listing impressions, reviews. Handoff: `/gtm competitors` for how rivals rank there.
- **Engineering as marketing** - Week 1: define the smallest tool that answers one searched question; scope to days, not weeks. Weekly loop: build, then seed it where the ICP asks that question. Indicators: tool users, links earned, email captures. Handoff: `/gtm leadmagnet` (the tool is a capture asset - design the flow before building).
- **Email marketing** - Week 1: capture path and welcome email in place. Weekly loop: one useful send; grow the list via the feeding channel. Indicators: list growth, open/reply rates. Handoff: `/gtm emails` and `/gtm leadmagnet`.
- **Paid acquisition** - Do not start here without the readiness verdict. Handoff: `/gtm ads` - its gate decides whether this family is even scoreable yet, and its report owns budgets, kill criteria, and creative.
- **Partnerships & affiliates** - Week 1: list 10 adjacent audiences and the value each gets; draft one concrete offer. Weekly loop: 3-5 warm approaches, worked like founder-led sales. Indicators: conversations started, one pilot agreed. No dedicated command owns this family - keep it manual and log outcomes in `LOG.md`.
- **PR, events & speaking** - Week 1: the one genuinely newsworthy angle, written as a pitch. Weekly loop: 5 targeted pitches or one talk proposal. Indicators: responses, placements, ICP signups traced. Handoff: `/gtm position` if the story isn't sharp enough to pitch.
